🏆 Hall of Legends

The heavyweights that every "best of all time" list keeps coming back to.

1972🌹

The Godfather

Crime · Drama

The Godfather

Dir. Francis Ford Coppola · Crime Drama
★★★★★

The Corleone crime dynasty passes from aging patriarch Vito (Marlon Brando) to reluctant son Michael (Al Pacino), who is slowly consumed by the family business.

🎬 Won Best Picture at the Oscars and sits near the top of IMDb's all-time chart.
2008–2019🛡️

Marvel Cinematic Universe

Superhero · Franchise

The MCU

Starts with Iron Man (2008) · Shared universe
★★★★☆

Dozens of interconnected films built over years, climaxing in the Infinity Saga: Iron Man, Captain America, Thor and the Avengers versus Thanos.

🎬 Avengers: Endgame (2019) became one of the highest-grossing films ever made.
2009🌿

Avatar

Sci-Fi · Adventure

Avatar

Dir. James Cameron · Sci-Fi
★★★★☆

A paralysed marine is sent to the moon Pandora, where he inhabits a Na'vi body and ends up defending the natives from his own side.

🎬 Its 3D tech and visuals reshaped blockbuster filmmaking. Sequel: The Way of Water (2022).
2010🌀

Inception

Sci-Fi · Heist

Inception

Dir. Christopher Nolan · Sci-Fi Thriller
★★★★★

Dom Cobb (Leonardo DiCaprio) steals secrets from dreams, and is offered one last job: plant an idea inside a target's mind.

🎬 That spinning-top ending is still debated today.
2023☢️

Oppenheimer

Biography · Drama

Oppenheimer

Dir. Christopher Nolan · Biopic
★★★★★

Cillian Murphy plays J. Robert Oppenheimer, the physicist who led the Manhattan Project, and the political fallout that followed.

🎬 Won seven Oscars including Best Picture, Best Director and Best Actor.

🦇 Christian Bale's Batman

Christopher Nolan's Dark Knight Trilogy: a gritty, grounded take on Gotham.

Batman Begins · 2005
Bruce Wayne trains with the League of Shadows and returns to Gotham to forge the Batman. Villains: Ra's al Ghul and Scarecrow.
The Dark Knight · 2008
Batman, Gordon and Harvey Dent face the Joker. Heath Ledger's performance won a posthumous Oscar and the film is a staple of IMDb's top 5.
The Dark Knight Rises · 2012
Eight years later, a broken Bruce must return to stop Bane from destroying Gotham. The epic conclusion of the trilogy.

2008🃏

The Dark Knight

Crime · Superhero

The Dark Knight

Dir. Christopher Nolan · Christian Bale, Heath Ledger
★★★★★

A crime thriller dressed as a superhero film: chaos versus order, with Gotham's soul at stake.

🎬 Parts were shot with IMAX cameras, a first for a major feature.

🇯🇵 Anime & Japanese Cinema

From kaiju satire to quiet village dramas.

2016🦖

Shin Godzilla

Kaiju · Satire

Shin Godzilla

Dir. Hideaki Anno & Shinji Higuchi
★★★★☆

Godzilla rises from Tokyo Bay while the government drowns in committees and paperwork. Equal parts monster movie and bureaucratic satire.

🎬 Anno depicted the true meaning of "the God of Destruction, Gojira".
2004🍵

The Taste of Tea

Quirky · Family

The Taste of Tea (茶の味)

Dir. Katsuhito Ishii
★★★★☆

A surreal, gentle look at the Haruno family in the countryside: first love, giant imaginary figures and daily oddities.

🎬 Slow, funny, warm. A great comfort watch and Hideaki Anno made a cameo in this film.
2016🤟

A Silent Voice

Anime · Drama

A Silent Voice (聲の形)

Dir. Naoko Yamada · Kyoto Animation
★★★★★

A former bully seeks redemption by reconnecting with the deaf girl he tormented in grade school. A film about guilt, forgiveness and communication.

🎬 Based on the manga by Yoshitoki Ōima.
2025🗡️

Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle

Anime · Action

Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle

Studio Ufotable · Dir. Haruo Sotozaki
★★★★★

Tanjiro and the Demon Slayer Corps fall into Muzan's shifting fortress for the climactic final-arc battles against the Upper Moons.

🎬 Ufotable's fluid combat animation is the main draw.
2007🌾

A Gentle Breeze in the Village

Coming-of-age

A Gentle Breeze in the Village

Dir. Nobuhiro Yamashita
★★★★☆

A transfer student arrives at a tiny rural school with only a handful of classmates. Nothing "happens", and that's the charm: summer, friendship, small-town life.

🎬 Yamashita also directed Linda Linda Linda.

🎞️ 10 More Greats, Dissected

Other consistent heavyweights of all-time lists.

1994⛓️

The Shawshank Redemption

Drama

The Shawshank Redemption

Dir. Frank Darabont
★★★★★

Banker Andy Dufresne is sentenced to life in prison and quietly builds hope and a friendship with fellow inmate Red.

🎬 Long ranked #1 on IMDb's Top 250.
1994💼

Pulp Fiction

Crime · Dark comedy

Pulp Fiction

Dir. Quentin Tarantino
★★★★★

Intertwined stories of hitmen, a boxer and a gangster's wife told out of order, packed with iconic dialogue.

🎬 Won the Palme d'Or at Cannes.
1993🕯️

Schindler's List

History · Drama

Schindler's List

Dir. Steven Spielberg
★★★★★

Businessman Oskar Schindler saves over a thousand Jewish people by employing them in his factories during the Holocaust.

🎬 Mostly shot in black and white, and won Best Picture.
1957⚖️

12 Angry Men

Courtroom · Drama

12 Angry Men

Dir. Sidney Lumet
★★★★★

A single juror doubts a guilty verdict and forces eleven others to re-examine the case, all inside one jury room.

🎬 Proof that great cinema only needs a room and a script.
2003💍

LOTR: Return of the King

Fantasy · Epic

The Return of the King

Dir. Peter Jackson
★★★★★

Frodo and Sam near Mount Doom while Aragorn leads the armies of Men in the final stand against Sauron.

🎬 Won all 11 Oscars it was nominated for.
1999🧼

Fight Club

Thriller · Satire

Fight Club

Dir. David Fincher
★★★★☆

A sleepless office worker and soap salesman Tyler Durden start an underground fight club that spirals into something bigger.

🎬 Based on Chuck Palahniuk's novel. First rule: you know the rest.
2001🐉

Spirited Away

Anime · Fantasy

Spirited Away

Dir. Hayao Miyazaki · Studio Ghibli
★★★★★

Ten-year-old Chihiro wanders into a spirit world and must work at a bathhouse for gods to free her parents.

🎬 Won the Oscar for Best Animated Feature.
2019🏠

Parasite

Thriller · Dark comedy

Parasite

Dir. Bong Joon-ho
★★★★★

A poor family cons its way into the lives of a wealthy household, until the class divide turns violent.

🎬 First non-English film to win Best Picture at the Oscars.
2014🪐

Interstellar

Sci-Fi · Drama

Interstellar

Dir. Christopher Nolan
★★★★★

With Earth dying, a former pilot leads a crew through a wormhole searching for a new home, torn between mission and his daughter.

🎬 Physicist Kip Thorne advised on the black hole visuals.
2016☄️

Your Name.

Anime · Romance

Your Name. (君の名は。)

Dir. Makoto Shinkai
★★★★★

A country girl and a Tokyo boy mysteriously swap bodies, and then a comet changes everything.

🎬 Became one of the highest-grossing anime films ever.
